# Stage 15 — Strategic Impact Analysis Engine

## 1. Overview & Objectives

The Strategic Impact Analysis subsystem systematically evaluates how external developments (geopolitical conflicts, subsidy policies, regulatory shifts, technological breakthroughs) impact an enterprise across **10 core dimensions**.

Rather than outputting generic prose, each assessment evaluates direction, magnitude, timeframe, entity exposure, and explicitly distinguishes between public intelligence and internal corporate financials.

---

## 2. The 10 Strategic Impact Dimensions

| Dimension | Scope & Focus | Typical Indicators |
|---|---|---|
| `geopolitical` | Cross-border trade disputes, regional conflicts, national security policies | Export controls, tariffs, port blockades |
| `macroeconomic` | Inflation, interest rates, currency volatility, sovereign debt | Central bank policy rates, FX rates, GDP deflators |
| `regulatory` | Antitrust, environmental mandates, AI sovereignty, data privacy | Legislation drafts, enforcement actions, compliance fines |
| `supply_chain` | Component availability, single-source dependency, freight congestion | Shipping lead times, factory shutdowns, port throughput |
| `financial_liquidity` | Capital availability, debt refinancing, working capital constraints | Credit spreads, commercial paper yields, capex budgets |
| `competitive_landscape`| Market share shifts, competitor M&A, aggressive capacity additions | Pricing changes, patent filings, tender wins |
| `technological_disruption`| Obsolete architectures, new computing paradigms, AI autonomy | Benchmarks, breakthrough publications, patents |
| `reputational` | ESG controversies, public scrutiny, ethics investigations | Media sentiment, NGO reports, social intelligence |
| `operational` | Labor disputes, IT outages, facility qualification delays | Strike actions, cloud downtime, plant audits |
| `cyber_physical_security`| Critical infrastructure attacks, espionage, ransomware, IP theft | CVE notices, state-sponsored intrusion alerts |

---

## 3. Rating & Evaluation Scale

### Potential Direction:
- `positive`: Net upside vector expanding margins, market share, or access.
- `negative`: Adverse exposure creating friction, costs, or delays.
- `neutral`: Structural rebalancing with minimal net enterprise impact.
- `bifurcated`: Mixed effects (e.g., benefits domestic operations while disrupting overseas export units).

### Estimated Magnitude:
- `low`: Minor operational friction (<2% margin or revenue impact).
- `moderate`: Notable budget or scheduling variance (2%–5% impact).
- `severe`: Substantial operational disruption or capital reallocation (5%–15% impact).
- `transformative`: Fundamental shift in business model, market access, or viability (>15% impact).

### Time Horizon:
- `immediate_30d`: Active impact occurring within 0–30 calendar days.
- `near_term_90d`: Developing impact expected within 30–90 calendar days.
- `medium_term_12m`: Strategic shift unfolding across 3–12 months.
- `long_term_24m_plus`: Structural multi-year reallocation (12–24+ months).

---

## 4. Public Intelligence Disclosure Guardrail

Where an enterprise has not linked internal ERP or financial accounting integrations:
- Assessments explicitly append the notice:
  > *"Strategic impact assessments correlate public intelligence disclosures, trade filings, and verified signals. Where private internal financials are unlinked, assessments strictly disclose public origin rather than fabricating synthetic balance sheets."*
- Prohibits fabricated financial figures (e.g. *"This will cause an exact \$12.4M drop in Q3 EBITDA"*) unless supported by explicit enterprise disclosures.
